✦   Chapter I — Written With Love & Honesty   ✦

Dear Aastha,

Ab tum apne life me aage bado, don't think about me.

Pata nahi is letter ki shuruaat kaise karun. Bahut baar socha ki likhun ya na likhun. Lekin dil ki kuch baatein aisi hoti hain jo keh dena hi behtar hota hai. Isliye aaj bina kisi shikayat ke, sirf apni kahani likh raha hoon.

Mujhe aaj bhi yaad nahi ki hum pehli baar kab mile the. Kaise ek anjaan insaan dheere-dheere meri aadat ban gaya. Kab tum sirf ek dost se badhkar meri har roz ki zarurat ban gayi, mujhe khud bhi pata nahi chala.

Humari kahani kisi perfect love story jaisi kabhi nahi thi.

Sach kahun, to agar koi humari story ko dekhe to shayad bole ki humne 80% waqt sirf ladte hue hi guzara. Gussa, ego, misunderstandings, block, unblock... ye sab hi to tha humare beech.

Isi liye maine tumhara naam "Fighter" rakha tha.

Sirf contact list me nahi... apne dil me bhi.

Tum hamesha meri Fighter rahogi.

Maine tumhe na jaane kitni baar block kiya hoga. Ek-do baar nahi... shayad pachaas baar se bhi zyada. Aur har baar tumne hi mujhe mana liya. Aaj sochta hoon to lagta hai ki agar tumhare andar itna attachment na hota, to shayad humari kahani bahut pehle hi khatam ho gayi hoti.

Logon ko lagta hoga ki maine tumhe kisi aur ke liye block kiya hoga.

Lekin sach ye hai...

Maine kabhi kisi teesre insaan ki wajah se tumhe block nahi kiya.

Mera frustration bas itna tha ki jab mujhe tumhari zarurat hoti thi, tum available nahi hoti thi.

Main sirf itna chahta tha ki mere message ka reply thoda jaldi aa jaye... meri call receive ho jaye... jab main akela feel kar raha hoon, tab tum bas ek baar "haan bolo" keh do.

Lekin tum shayad is baat ki gehraai kabhi samajh hi nahi paayi.

Tumhare liye ye chhoti si baat thi...

Mere liye wahi sabse badi baat thi.

Har baar isi baat par ladai hoti rahi. Har baar main gussa hota raha. Aur har baar tum mana leti thi.

Dheere-dheere mujhe bhi ladne ki aadat ho gayi. Mujhe laga ki shayad humara pyaar isi tarah chalega. Lekin tab tak bahut der ho chuki thi.

Ek waqt tha jab tum fight karna chahti thi kyunki tumhe lagta tha ki relationship bach jayega. Phir ek waqt aaya... jab tumhe sirf pyaar chahiye tha. Care... Respect... Understanding...

Aur sach kahun...

Main tumhe wo sab de hi nahi paaya.

Main ladte-ladte itna badal gaya tha ki mujhe apni hi aadaton ka ehsaas nahi raha.

Bahut baar main kehta tha...

"Diploma ke baad main tumse baat nahi karunga."

Tum shayad sochti hogi ki mujhe farq nahi padta. Lekin wo jhooth tha. Sach to ye tha ki mujhe future se darr lagta tha. Mujhe lagta tha jis wajah se hum connected hain, diploma khatam hote hi wo wajah bhi khatam ho jayegi. Main future ke baare me sochkar hi toot jaata tha.

Isliye jaanbujhkar aisi baatein bolta tha taaki tum samajh jao... Lekin tum udaas ho jaati thi. Aur us waqt main apni saanse rok leta tha, sirf isliye taaki tum meri awaaz me chhupa dard na sun pao.

Maine kabhi apne emotions dikhana nahi seekha. Bachpan se bas ek baat suni thi...

"Ladke zyada emotional nahi hote."

Aur maine us baat ko sach maan liya. Isliye tumne hamesha mera gussa dekha, meri khamoshi dekhi... lekin mere aansu kabhi nahi dekhe.

Kabhi-kabhi main sochta hoon—main hamesha tumhari galtiyan hi kyun ginta raha? Kyun maaf karna nahi seekha? Shayad problem tum nahi...

Main khud tha.

Maine kabhi tumhari respect nahi ki, kabhi tumhari value nahi samjhi, aur kabhi tumse us tarah baat nahi ki jis tarah tum deserve karti thi. Hamesha gussa, attitude, aur ego. Aur shayad isi wajah se tum kahin aur wo cheezein dhoondhne lagi jo tumhe mujhse milni chahiye thi.

Aur aaj mujhe is baat ka bilkul bura nahi lagta, balki khushi hoti hai kyuki tumne apni life me aage badhne ka faisla kiya.

Tum mujhe kabhi-kabhi kehti thi...

"Aap bahut achhe ho."

Main kabhi react nahi karta tha, kyuki mujhe compliments se darr lagta tha. Main pehle hi dard se bachna chahta tha. Lekin jitna door rehna chaha, utna hi tumhari aadat lagti gayi. Tumhari baatein, tumhari harkatein, tumhara gussa—sab meri zindagi ka hissa ban gaya.

Tum meri aadat thi. Aur tumhe... mere bina jeene ki aadat ho chuki thi.

Log meri khamoshi ko attitude samajhte hain. Lekin us khamoshi ke andar itne emotions chhupe hain ki agar ek din sab keh diya, to shayad tum bhi ro padogi. Aur main tumhe dobara rulana nahi chahta.

Main karma par maanta hoon. Jo dard maine diya hai, wo kabhi na kabhi mujhe bhi milega, aur shayad mil bhi raha hai.

Ab mujhe tumse ek hi cheez chahiye:

Mujhe mat manao. Mujhe mat roko. Mujhe mat dhoondo.

Bas apni life me aage badh jao. Kisi aise insaan ko chunna jo mere jaisa kabhi na ho—jo tumhari respect kare, tumhe samjhe, tumhari care kare, aur tumhe itna pyaar de jitna dard maine diya.

Main chahta hoon tumhare naye chapter me mera gussa na ho, meri ladai na ho...

Sirf tumhari khushi ho.

01-07-2026 — Ye sirf ek date nahi, ye humari kahani ka aakhri panna hai.

Aur agar kabhi zindagi ke kisi mod par meri yaad aaye, to bas itna yaad rakhna...

Ek ladka tha jo pyaar bahut karta tha... bas dikhana kabhi nahi seekh paaya.

01-07-2026
Hamesha yaad rahogi...
Love you... suwar, my Fighter.
Prem

✦   The Final Epilogue — One Last Time   ✦

☾   30 October  •  Time: 9:28 PM
Ek Aakhiri Baar...

Ye woh waqt hai jab main hum dono ki kahani ko ek aakhiri baar continue kar raha hoon.

27 October — woh din tha jab maine bina kuch kahe, bina aakhiri baar baat kiye, tumhe block kar diya tha. Aur sach kahun toh mujhe aaj bhi is baat ka dukh hai. Shayad maine aisa isliye kiya kyunki jis insaan se main kabhi baat kiya karta tha, woh ab pehle jaisi nahi rahi thi. Woh insaan, jise mere block karne se farq padta tha, jo kisi na kisi tarah mujhe mana hi leti thi, shayad ab woh kahin kho gayi hai.

Tumne hamesha mera gussa dekha, meri narazgi dekhi, mera badla hua behaviour dekha. Lekin shayad kabhi us insaan ko nahi dekha jo tumhare liye chupchaap efforts karta tha, jo tumhe importance deta tha aur tumhari chhoti-chhoti baaton ko bhi yaad rakhta tha.

Aaj bhi jab main humare purane palon ko yaad karta hoon, toh chehre par ek halki si smile aa jaati hai. Na koi shikayat hai, na koi gila. Bas kuch yaadein hain, jinhe main chaah kar bhi bhula nahi sakta.

Main dil se chahta hoon ki tumhe woh sab kuch mile jo tum chahti ho. Tum khush raho, apni life mein aage badho aur woh sab haasil karo jiske tumne kabhi sapne dekhe the.

Ab shayad mere paas kehne ke liye aur kuch nahi hai. Kuch kahaniyan adhuri reh jaati hain, aur kuch log yaadon ka hissa ban kar reh jaate hain.

Goodbye! And thank you for being a part of my life.

Tum meri kahani ka ek khoobsurat hissa thi, aur hamesha rahogi.

30 October • 9:28 PM
